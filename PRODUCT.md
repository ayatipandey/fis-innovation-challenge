# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Pinned by the brief: React + TypeScript + Vite + React Router frontend; Node.js + Express app backend (auth, sessions, preferences, catalog, favorites, history, orchestration); Python FastAPI recommendation service (ranking, explanations, eligibility, points/miles value). SQLite for the local prototype.

## Users

Everyday consumers who hold points, miles, cashback and brand offers across several programs and lose value because rewards are scattered, conditions are confusing, and redemption takes too many steps. Secondary audience for this build: the student innovation-challenge judges and group members evaluating the prototype.

## Product Purpose

Perkly is an AI-powered reward discovery, optimization and redemption layer. It shows the right reward, explains its conditions clearly, and lets the user use it with one action, including split payments (rewards + money). Success: a user can go from "what should I care about today?" to a clearly understood, demo-redeemed reward on one screen.

Tagline: All your perks. One place.

## Positioning

Not a bank, card issuer, or loyalty program, and it issues no points currency. It is the intelligent layer between consumers, participating brands, existing reward programs and supported payment methods. Its differentiator is one-screen redemption: benefit, cost, validity, exclusions, how-to-use, payment choice and the "Use Reward" action live on the same screen, with transparent "why this reward" explanations that rank usefulness over the biggest advertised discount.

## Operating Context

Daily routines: morning coffee, groceries, restaurants, travel planning. Mobile-first usage at the point of purchase (Apple Pay, saved card, in-store coupon, cash), plus desktop for planning and comparing redemptions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Student prototype. No registered company yet; business details (group/entity name, contact email, address) are bracketed placeholders the group will fill in.
- Legal pages (Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Refunds, Accessibility) are written around United States law (incl. CCPA/CPRA-style rights) and are drafts, not legal advice.
- All brands, offers, balances, transactions and redemptions are synthetic demo data and must be labeled as such. No live brand partnerships.
- Payments and redemptions are simulated; no real card numbers or CVVs are ever collected.
- The recommendation engine is a transparent rule-based scorer, not a trained model; the Copilot is rule-based unless a language model is configured, and must say so.

## Brand Commitments

- Name Perkly; tagline "All your perks. One place."; primary action label "Use Reward".
- Brief-pinned palette family: warm ivory/off-white, deep espresso/charcoal, muted forest green, burgundy/wine, warm taupe/stone, restrained champagne/antique gold accents only (never for small essential text).
- Brief-pinned type direction: elegant editorial serif for large headings; highly readable sans for UI, forms and conditions.
- Personality: luxurious but approachable, intelligent not technical, calm, trustworthy, editorial, personal without being invasive.
- Reference for layout language only (not branding): Dribbble "AI-Powered Period Tracker Mobile App".

## Evidence on Hand

None. No customers, testimonials, statistics, press, or partnerships exist. Never fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the right reward, explain it clearly, make using it effortless.
2. Honesty over polish: unknown is shown as unknown; demo is labeled demo.
3. Usefulness beats headline discount; extra-spend requirements are warned about.
4. The user controls preferences and permissions; location is always optional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target: contrast, keyboard operation, screen-reader labels, large text, high contrast and reduced-motion settings editable in-app.
