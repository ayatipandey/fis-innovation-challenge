# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Pinned by the brief: React + TypeScript + Vite + React Router frontend; Node.js + Express app backend (auth, sessions, preferences, catalog, favorites, history, orchestration); Python FastAPI recommendation service (ranking, explanations, rule-based Copilot). SQLite for the local prototype.

## Users

Everyday consumers who want good offers from the brands they use, but find them scattered across apps, emails and codes, with confusing conditions and too many steps to use them. Secondary audience for this build: the student innovation-challenge judges and group members evaluating the prototype.

## Product Purpose

Perkly is a free membership network. Partner brands offer rewards to Perkly members; Perkly's recommendation engine shows each member the rewards that fit them, explains every condition clearly in one pop-up, and hands off to the brand's site (code applied) or shows a QR code for in-store use. Success: a member goes from "what should I care about today?" to a clearly understood reward used online or in store, in seconds.

Tagline: All your perks. One place.

## Positioning

Comparable to a member discount network (like UNiDAYS) but open to everyone, with AI-ranked recommendations and every condition (what you get, what you pay, valid, not valid, how to use) shown before use. Not a bank, payment processor or points program: members pay brands directly and Perkly issues no points. Revenue model: partner commissions or fees, disclosed in the product, never affecting rankings.

## Operating Context

Daily routines: morning coffee, groceries, restaurants, travel planning. Mobile-first usage at the point of purchase (showing a QR code in store, or tapping through to a brand's site), plus desktop for browsing and comparing rewards.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Student prototype. No registered company yet; business details (group/entity name, contact email, address) are bracketed placeholders the group will fill in.
- Legal pages (Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Refunds, Accessibility) are written around United States law (incl. CCPA/CPRA-style rights) and are drafts, not legal advice. Commission relationships must be disclosed (FTC).
- All partner brands, rewards, savings and purchase reports are synthetic demo data and must be labeled as such. No real brand partnerships.
- Perkly never takes payment. Online use hands off to the partner's site (simulated); in-store use shows a member code and QR code.
- The recommendation engine is a transparent rule-based scorer, not a trained model; the Copilot is rule-based unless a language model is configured, and must say so.
- Decided (Oct 2026): no points, no loyalty-program linking, no split payments.

## Brand Commitments

- Name Perkly; tagline "All your perks. One place."; primary action label "Use Reward" ("Use Reward online" / "Use Reward in store" when both apply).
- Brief-pinned palette family: warm ivory/off-white, deep espresso/charcoal, muted forest green, burgundy/wine, warm taupe/stone, restrained champagne/antique gold accents only (never for small essential text).
- Brief-pinned type direction: elegant editorial serif for large headings; highly readable sans for UI, forms and conditions.
- Personality: luxurious but approachable, intelligent not technical, calm, trustworthy, editorial, personal without being invasive.
- Reference for layout language only (not branding): Dribbble "AI-Powered Period Tracker Mobile App".

## Evidence on Hand

None. No customers, testimonials, statistics, press, or partnerships exist. Never fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the right reward, explain it clearly, make using it effortless.
2. Honesty over polish: unknown is shown as unknown; demo is labeled demo; commissions are disclosed.
3. Usefulness beats headline discount; extra-spend requirements are warned about.
4. The user controls preferences and permissions; location is always optional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target: contrast, keyboard operation, screen-reader labels, large text, high contrast and reduced-motion settings editable in-app.
