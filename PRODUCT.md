# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (a preview of the planned phone app)

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Vite frontend; Node + Express API on libSQL (Turso when deployed); Python FastAPI AI service (uplift model, moment engine, Perkly's Pilot). Deployed on Vercel.

## Users

Everyone with points they never get around to using: bank card rewards, coffee stamp cards, store memberships. University students are the demo persona (Maya). Secondary audience: innovation-challenge judges and partner programs, who see the /insights page.

## Product Purpose

Perkly links read-only to existing programs, turns balances into concrete rewards with a dollar value, picks the right moment to suggest one, and redeems in one tap with a 2-hour hold that prevents double spending. It learns from ratings and proves its lift against a control group.

Pitch: Other apps show you your points. Perkly spends them for you at the right moment.

## Positioning

Not a loyalty program and not a wallet of balances: Perkly never issues or holds points. Its difference is the decision: which reward, and when. An uplift model sends a notification only when it would change behavior.

## Operating Context

At the point of intent: walking past a shop, at lunch, heading home. Mobile-first, with desktop for planning in Explore and the Pilot.
